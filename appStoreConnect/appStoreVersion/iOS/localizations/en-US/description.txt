Time, on your side.

Meet SpotClock 2.0: a considered parking companion with a clear countdown, your saved spot, and a simpler way back to your car.

SET YOUR STAY
Choose a quick duration or set your own. Add a note for the level, bay, or nearby landmark, and start your parking timer.

KEEP TIME IN SIGHT
See your countdown and return time at a glance. Parking alarms help you keep track of your stay. Need longer? Extend your reminder in a tap.

REMEMBER YOUR SPOT
Your current location is saved when you start. Open your parking map, check walking directions, and see a suggested leave-by time based on your walk back.

GET TO KNOW YOUR ROUTINE
Browse past stays, revisit saved locations, and explore parking insights and duration suggestions based on your own history.

MADE FOR IPHONE DUO
On the open display, your timer and parking map share the space. Fold it closed and keep the essentials close at hand. Designed for iOS 27.1, with support for larger text, VoiceOver, and light and dark appearances.

SPOTCLOCK PRO
Start with three free timers per month. Subscribe to Pro for unlimited parking timers. Plans and current prices are shown in the app. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through your App Store account.

SpotClock is a parking reminder. It does not pay or extend your parking meter. Location and alarm permissions are needed for the corresponding features.
